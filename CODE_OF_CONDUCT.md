# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Participation in this project should be respectful, constructive and focused on learning and engineering quality.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Provide actionable, technically grounded feedback.
- Welcome questions from people with different experience levels.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not individuals.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing and publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibility

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions that do not meet these expectations.
